## Team

Depending on the date, you will host **1 to 4 people**.

## Passes & guest list

The promoter agrees to provide **4 AAA passes** for the artist's team and **5 guest list spots** for the artist's guests.

## Travel & accommodation

The team usually travels by **5-seat car**. Please provide a **free, secure parking space** close to the venue and to the accommodation.

If the OBSIMO team travels by train or plane, please contact us to arrange transfers: station/airport → accommodation, accommodation → venue, venue → accommodation, accommodation → station/airport.

**Rooming list:** 2 twin rooms with breakfast.

## Catering

**In the dressing room:**

- Fresh fruit
- A bottle of whisky — *no Jack Daniel's*
- Coke Zero — *cans or glass bottles preferred*
- A photo of Mr. Bean

> Please avoid single-use plastic bottles and packaging.

**Meals:** please provide **4 vegetarian meals** for dinner, and **4 vegetarian meals** for lunch if load-in is before 2 pm.

No allergies.

## Merchandising

The merch is mainly **vinyl records and goodies**.

Please provide a space of **at least 2 m²** accessible to the public, with tables, chairs, lighting and power outlets.

## Soundcheck

Please allow **at least 50 minutes** for setup and soundcheck.

## Provided by the venue

- **2 flightcases** to hold Obsimo's gear
  - flightcase #1: *≈ 90–110 cm high / ≈ 100–120 cm wide*
  - flightcase #2: *≈ 90–110 cm high / ≈ 60–80 cm wide*
- **2 active mono DIs** or **1 active stereo DI** (Radial preferred)
- **4 × 230V outlets**
- **2 monitors**

*If the venue has no flightcases available, flightcase #1 can be replaced by a riser (with black skirt) and flightcase #2 by a keyboard stand.*

*The gear can be stacked on a rolling riser to make changeovers easier.*

## Lighting

The overall mood of the live show is **"Dark & Dynamic"**.

That means: **no front light**, so the artist stays a black silhouette throughout the set.

- **Quiet moments:** dark, intimate atmosphere, very little light, haze recommended.
- **Dance moments:** very dynamic light movement, strobes and the like.

Thanks in advance :)
